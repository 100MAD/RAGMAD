# Northwind University Library Guide

## Hours and locations

The main library is open Monday through Thursday from 08:00 to 22:00. On Friday it closes at 18:00. Saturday hours are 10:00 to 16:00. The library is closed on Sunday and on public holidays.

The quiet reading room is on the second floor and stays open with the main building. Group study rooms 201, 202, and 203 are on the same floor. Each group room seats six people. A room can be booked for up to three hours per group per day.

The special collections room is on the fourth floor, room 410. It is open Tuesday and Thursday from 13:00 to 17:00. Visitors must leave bags in the lockers beside the elevator.

## Borrowing

Undergraduate students may borrow up to 10 books for 21 days. Postgraduate students may borrow up to 20 books for 42 days. Academic staff may borrow up to 40 books for 90 days.

Journals, reference books marked with a red sticker, and items from special collections cannot be borrowed. Laptops from the laptop locker may be borrowed for 4 hours and cannot leave the building.

A book can be renewed twice unless another reader has reserved it. Renewals are done in the catalogue or at the service desk.

## Fines and reservations

The overdue fine is 0.50 euros per book per day, up to a maximum of 15 euros per book. When unpaid fines reach 10 euros, borrowing is blocked until the balance is paid at the service desk. The library does not accept cash. Payment is by campus card only.

A reader may place up to 5 reservations at a time. When a reserved book is returned, the library holds it for 3 days. The reader is notified by email.

## Interlibrary loans

An interlibrary loan costs 8 euros for a book and 4 euros for a journal article. Requests are submitted through the catalogue form named "Request from another library". Delivery usually takes 7 to 10 working days. The loan period for an interlibrary book is 14 days and it cannot be renewed.

## Course reserves

Course reserve books are kept behind the service desk. The loan period is 3 hours, and the book must stay inside the library. A student card is left at the desk while the book is in use.

## Printing and computers

Printing costs 0.05 euros per black-and-white page and 0.20 euros per colour page. The print credit is loaded onto the campus card. Computers in the first-floor lab require a student login. The lab closes 30 minutes before the building closes.
