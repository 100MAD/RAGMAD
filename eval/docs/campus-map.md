# Northwind Campus Map Notes

## Buildings

The library faces the east gate. The science block is north of the library and is labelled building S. The student centre is west of the library and is labelled building C. The rector's office is in building A, which is the stone building on the south side of the main lawn.

## Getting around

Bicycles must be parked in the racks beside the east gate. Bicycles left against the library steps are removed every day at 18:00. The campus shuttle leaves the east gate every 20 minutes between 08:00 and 19:00 and stops at the science block and the railway station.

## Rooms

Lecture hall L1 is on the ground floor of the science block and seats 180 people. The campus clinic is in building C, room 12, and is open on weekdays from 09:00 to 15:00. The visitor car park is south of building A and costs 2 euros per hour.
